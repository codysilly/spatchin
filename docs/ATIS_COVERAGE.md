# C070 ATIS acquisition plan — draft, 2026-11-25

Target: all 130 C070 airports. Candidate evidence: https://weather.schaffrath.net/D-ATIS.html (75 station identifiers linked to atis.info, checked 2026-11-25). Intersection with verified registry: 62 candidates; 68 unlisted and not configured. This is not a provider guarantee or proof that unlisted stations lack D-ATIS. The existing three stations and MEM are in the candidate set.

No published atis.info bulk API, limit or redistribution permission was established in the public documentation search. Do not infer permission from other clients. ATIS Relay documents a paid all-stations endpoint (https://atisrelay.com/api_docs); it is not authorized and has separate terms. FAA SWIFT/SCDS (https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/technology/swim/products/get_connected) is a possible subscription path; specific service access/terms and credentials require separate review.

Proposed existing-source implementation: one single-station request per candidate per execution, up to 62; no retries, sequential requests, stop on HTTP 429/503, stop starting new requests after 180 seconds, per-request socket timeout at most 20 seconds and bounded by remaining budget. Existing five-minute Actions timeout remains the outer limit; socket timeout is not a strict whole-response deadline. Rotate start station hourly to reduce starvation, not guarantee complete coverage under slow responses. No browser/provider fan-out. Retain prior reports on partial failure. Snapshot configured_airports and catalog_airports identify all 130 dispositions; missing report is not a provider capability determination.

At hourly dispatch: up to 62 requests/hour or 1,488/day. With all six fallback cron offsets plus one hourly dispatch: 434/hour or 10,416/day, excluding additional dispatches. These are modeled exposure, not provider-approved quotas. Hourly collection cannot guarantee the existing 45-minute freshness threshold. Keep stale labels; do not change cadence or thresholds here.

Production activation is blocked pending provider usage/redistribution evidence and explicit owner authorization for the selected acquisition budget and Pages publication. No live endpoint probe, account creation or paid service is authorized by this draft. Full coverage requires resolving the 68 unknown stations or documenting why they cannot be collected; do not mark SPA-97 Done based on candidate list alone.
